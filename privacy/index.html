Episode Stats: Privacy Policy

Episode Stats is used only by its owner, with the owner's own TikTok account.

What it reads: basic profile information, account statistics, and the title, date and view, like, comment and share counts of public videos.

How it is used: only to send the owner daily statistics in a private Telegram chat. Data is not sold or used for advertising.

Where it is kept: access tokens and statistics are stored on the owner's private server. Statistics messages also pass through Telegram.

Removal: access can be revoked at any time in TikTok's settings. The owner can delete the stored data from the server.

This website itself uses no cookies or analytics.
